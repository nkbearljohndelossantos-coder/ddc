# DCC ENTERPRISE PLATFORM — USER GUIDE

## 1. Getting Started
1. Access the web interface at `https://dcc.enterprise.corp`.
2. Log in using your corporate credentials.
3. Upon login, your active department context and available features will be automatically displayed.

## 2. Searching & Viewing Documents
- **Document List**: Navigate to *Documents* to view records belonging to your department.
- **Searchable PDF**: Click *PDF* on any completed record to download the full OCR searchable PDF.
- **QC Review**: Reviewers can access *QC Workspace* to inspect low-confidence pages and submit corrections.

## 3. Workflows & Tasks
- Claim and complete approval tasks assigned to your role or department.
- View workflow progression in real time.
