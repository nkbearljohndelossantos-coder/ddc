# DCC ENTERPRISE PLATFORM — ADMINISTRATOR GUIDE

## 1. Scanner Fleet Management
- Navigate to *Administration & Fleet* to inspect registered scanner hardware (Brother ADS-4300N).
- View online/offline status, IP addresses, and last received heartbeats.

## 2. Disaster Recovery & Backup Auditing
- View real-time observed RPO and observed RTO against enterprise targets (RPO: 60m, RTO: 30m).
- Review historical drill outcomes and trigger automated recovery sweeps.

## 3. Dead-Letter Recovery
- Inspect failed asynchronous webhook deliveries or notifications and trigger batch replays.
